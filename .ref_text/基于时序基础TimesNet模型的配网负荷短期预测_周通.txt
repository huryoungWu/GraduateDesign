基于时序基础 TimesNet 模型的配网负荷短期预测

周 通，康 健
（国网陕西省电力有限公司 西安供电公司，陕西 西安 710000）

摘要：针对配网负荷数据离散分布特征显著的问题，提出基于时序基础 TimesNet 模型的配网负荷

短期预测方法。通过 Rapid-MIC 计算负荷之间的相互作用程度，并将其量化结果作为配网负荷母

线邻接矩阵中的参数，从而得到配网负荷时序耦合关联的量化表征。设计了针对配网负荷数据的

时序基础 TimesNet 模型，该模型以时间块为基本单元，将输入的一维配网负荷时序耦合关联序列转

换至二维空间，利用 Inception 函数提取时间特征，再将其反向嵌入至一维空间，实现负荷参数时序变

化的聚合。实验结果表明，所提方法应用后负荷参数的误差基本控制在-40.0~60.0 MW 范围内，有

效反映了真实的负荷状况，证明了该方法在配网负荷短期预测中的可行性和准确性。

关键词：时序基础 TimesNet 模型；配网负荷；短期预测；时序耦合关联；二维张量

Short term load forecasting of distribution network based on time series TimesNet model
ZHOU Tong，KANG Jian （Xi’an Power Supply Company，State Grid Shaanxi Electric Power Co.，Ltd.，Xi’an 710000，China）
Abstract: Based on the significant discrete distribution characteristics of distribution network load data， a short⁃term load forecasting method for distribution network based on the TimesNet model is proposed. By using Rapid MIC to calculate the degree of interaction between loads and quantifying the results as parameters in the adjacency matrix of the distribution network load bus，a quantitative representation of the temporal coupling relationship of distribution network loads can be obtained. A TimesNet model based on time series was designed for distribution network load data. The model uses time blocks as the basic unit to transform the input one ⁃ dimensional distribution network load time series coupling correlation sequence into a two ⁃ dimensional space. The Inception function is used to extract time features，which are then reverse embedded into the one ⁃ dimensional space to aggregate the time series changes of load parameters. The experimental results show that the error distribution range of specific load parameters after the proposed method is basically controlled between - 40.0 MW and 60.0 MW， effectively reflecting the real load situation，and proving the feasibility and accuracy of this method in short⁃term load forecasting of distribution networks. Keywords: time series based TimesNet model；distribution network load；short term forecasting；temporal
coupling correlation；two⁃dimensional tensor

为了提高配网负荷短期预测的准确性和稳定 性，该文开展了基于时序基础 TimesNet 模型的配网 负 荷 短 期 预 测 研 究 ，以 期 实 现 对 负 荷 变 化 的 精 准 捕 捉和预测，为电力系统的调度、规划和控制提供更加 可靠的数据支持。
